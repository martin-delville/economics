I am a PhD candidate in economics at Télécom Paris and CREST, Institut Polytechnique de Paris. My research is in empirical industrial organization and digital economics. I study how regulation and communication networks shape markets and behavior: how a minimum delivery fee on online book sales affects bookstores and consumers, how fiber deployment affects local elections, and how France's first telecommunication network helped integrate its grain markets in the nineteenth century. In 2026, I was a visiting PhD student at Imperial College Business School.
